Axyl v1.5.1, built from source  ·  4 validators on one laptop  ·  quorum 3 of 4

If Rayls validators fail mid-payment, does the payment settle, stall, or fork?

A hands-on test of Axyl, the Rayls consensus client. Real crashes, real payments, raw data published.

It stalls. It does not fork.

  • One of four down: payments kept settling
  • Two of four down: chain stopped, payments waited, then all settled
  • A returned hash did not mean settled

One laptop, crashes only, small samples. Mainnet payments not tested.
